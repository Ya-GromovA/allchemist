# Methodist Review Checklist MVP

Status: Content-PROD-2 draft checklist.
Date: 2026-07-09

## Chemistry

- Verify reaction classification wording for school level.
- Check acids wording against selected curriculum and grade.
- Check metals/reactivity sequence framing.
- Verify Zn + HCl equation, observations and limits of student-facing explanation.
- Confirm all chemistry tasks have unambiguous expected answers.

## Physics

- Verify grade placement for uniformly accelerated motion.
- Check notation: `v`, `v0`, `a`, `t`, `s`.
- Confirm formulas are taught with correct assumptions and units.
- Check that Newton laws are presented only as a next topic, not as a full lesson.
- Confirm calculation tasks are age-appropriate.

## Biology

- Verify cell structure wording for 5-6 grade.
- Check plant cell structures and terminology.
- Verify onion epidermis observation claims and label confidence.
- Confirm microscope content distinguishes real observation from schematic simplification.

## Safety

- Chemistry: acids, Zn + HCl, hydrogen gas, glassware and PPE.
- Biology: slides, cover slips, stains, biological samples and microscope handling.
- Confirm no draft reads like an unsupervised home experiment.
- Confirm all safety-sensitive cards say teacher/local protocol is required.

## Age Adequacy

- Check vocabulary load.
- Check length and cognitive difficulty.
- Confirm examples use school-appropriate numbers and contexts.
- Ensure tasks do not require concepts not yet introduced.

## Copyright

- Confirm no textbook text, task wording, diagrams or lab procedures are copied.
- Confirm OpenStax/official-source references are used under the recorded license posture.
- Confirm attribution/legal review plan before any reuse of external text or images.
- Confirm textbook placeholders remain reference-only.

## Tasks

- Check correctness of expected answers.
- Check that prompts are clear and short.
- Check that task type matches the skill being assessed.
- Reject tasks that depend on missing diagrams/assets.

## Labs

- Require teacher-led status for chemistry demos.
- Require local safety protocol before operational lab steps.
- Confirm no quantities/procedure steps are published before safety approval.

## Simulations

- Verify formulas, units and parameter bounds.
- Confirm UI simulations cannot be confused with real lab instructions.
- Require engine scenario review before release.

## Microscope

- Verify specimen/image source and license.
- Check label confidence per magnification/preparation.
- Confirm safety notes for glass, stains and biological material.
- Require reviewed assets before student-facing microscope mode.
